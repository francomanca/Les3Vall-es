Flight & transfer logistics

Is the flight included in the € price? — No.
The Travelex € package covers the ski pass, 7 nights, the airport transfer and luggage transport. You buy the WizzAir Budapest–Torino round-trip yourself (base fare + small cabin bag only). Travelex ships your suitcase and ski gear from Budapest, so you don't check bags. Travelex reference RT fare (WizzAir, updated Sep 2026): 127 880 Ft (Jan 30) / 35 580 Ft (Mar 13).

Travelex explicitly states the Milano Malpensa outbound flight cannot be booked for 3 Vallées. By plane, Travelex only serves the 3 Vallées on Jan 30 – Feb 6 and Mar 13 – 20. All other Torino weeks go to other resorts, so these are your only two options.

Wellness spotlight — CGH Les Clarines Possible priority

If a proper post-ski wellness centre is a priority, Clarines is the strongest of the three — the CGH spa is a destination in itself.

  • Heated indoor pool, jacuzzi/hot tub, sauna & hammam, fitness room, billiards.
  • Extra-cost massages & facials on site — book a group slot mid-week.
  • Spacious modern Savoyard wood apartments; linens & towels included; bakery to the door.

Watch-outs: spa booked at reception (limited hours); WiFi can be weak. Sits at 1 850 m, quiet evenings.

Nightlife spotlight — La Folie Douce Val Thorens

Val Thorens hosts La Folie Douce, the legendary on-mountain après party — live DJs, cabaret and dancing in ski boots from early afternoon.

  • On the slopes above the resort, so it favours Les Balcons & Cheval Blanc — easy roll home after.
  • VT also has the wider late-night bar/club scene; Cheval Blanc is right in the thick of it.

From Les Menuires (Clarines) it's a daytime ski-over, not a walk-home option at night.

Why this one

  • • One apartment for the whole group (120 m², 12 people) — only Balcons offers true 8/10/12-person single units. Cheval Blanc maxes at 6p and Clarines' 8p is sold out, so both split you across separate flats. (Prefer to save? A Balcons 2× Óriási 6p split runs ≈ €942/pp — see the "Cheapest" tag.)
  • • Highest resort (2 300 m) — snow-sure even for a mid-March week.
  • • Ski-in/ski-out + spa + nightlife — pool/saunas/hot tubs on site, Intersport & supermarket in the building, Val Thorens après on the doorstep.
  • • March beats January — cheaper flight (91 300 Ft less/pp on WizzAir RT), cheaper rooms, and Jan 30 has the Balcons 12-person unit sold out.
If wellness is the group's priority
Switch to CGH Les Clarines ★★★★★ (Mar 13–20) — a 6+6 setup at ≈ €942/pp all-in (only Óriási 6p left this week; 4p/8p sold out). Best spa of the three (indoor pool, jacuzzi, sauna, hammam, gym + massage/facials) and calm evenings — trade-off is two apartments, 1 850 m, and no walk-home nightlife like La Folie Douce. Le Coeur des Loges ★★★★ is another Menuires ski-in option (see estimator). Cheval Blanc ★★★ is the budget/party pick at ≈ €877/pp, but small dated rooms split 6+6.
